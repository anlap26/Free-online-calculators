Other calculators

Calendar age, date arithmetic that clamps the end of the month, durations, a 4.0 GPA, a weighted grade, and unit conversions.

Age is the calendar gap from the date of birth to the date you pick: whole years, then whole months, then the remaining days. The total is also the count of midnights in UTC, so a daylight-saving change does not add or remove a day. A 29 February birthday reaches the next whole year on 1 March in a non-leap year, which is the first date the anniversary exists. Next birthday uses that same rule.

The date page reports days from the first date to the second, the count that includes both dates, and the same year-month-day gap. Adding a month to 31 January lands on the last day of February, 28 or 29, instead of spilling into March. Years are applied first, then months, then days. The time page adds or subtracts hours, minutes, and seconds, and it can measure from one clock time to another. An earlier end time is counted into the next day, and the result says so.

GPA uses one common US 4.0 map: A and A+ are 4.0, A− is 3.7, and the steps continue down to F at 0. It is credit-weighted. The grade page weights each score by the weight you type, or by 1 if you leave the weight blank, then maps 90, 80, 70, and 60 to A, B, C, and D. Unit conversion uses exact factors: 2.54 centimetres in an inch, 1.609344 kilometres in a mile, 0.45359237 kilograms in a pound, and 3.785411784 litres in a US gallon. Fahrenheit and Celsius meet at 32 and 212. A length is not converted into a weight.

Questions people ask

Does age count the birthday as already reached?

On the anniversary the year count increases and the months and days are zero. For a 29 February birthday in a non-leap year, that anniversary is 1 March.

Why does 31 January plus one month become the end of February?

February does not have a 31st. The page keeps the day inside the destination month instead of rolling into March.

Whose GPA scale is this?

A common US 4.0 scale where A+ does not go above 4.0 and A− is 3.7. Schools that use 4.3 for A+, or a different step, will not match. The page states the map it uses.
