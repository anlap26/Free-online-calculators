Roaming and eSIM calculator

Roaming is the number of days times the daily price you type. An eSIM total is the package price plus any gigabytes past the included amount, at the extra price you type.

A day count or an index is a planning aid. It is not a visa, a tax ruling, an insurance policy, or a quote.

The roaming and eSIM calculator compares two bills you describe yourself. The roaming total is the number of days multiplied by the daily roaming price. The eSIM total is the package price plus the extra data, and the extra data is the greater of zero and the gigabytes you expect to use minus the gigabytes included, multiplied by the price of an extra gigabyte. In short, anything inside the included amount adds nothing, and anything past it is charged at the extra price you typed. The page then subtracts the eSIM total from the roaming total and says which side is lower. If the two totals match, it says they match. The currency symbol is whatever you type. The page does not convert currencies.

An example that is cheaper only because of the numbers typed

Take 10 days at 8 per day. The roaming total is 80. Take a package priced at 25, with 5 GB included and 3 GB used. Used data is inside the included amount, so the extra is zero, and the extra price per gigabyte is not applied. The eSIM total is 25. Roaming minus eSIM is 55, so in this example the eSIM is cheaper by 55. That gap exists only because those were the numbers typed. The page has no view of a fair price, and it does not know Airalo's current price for any country. Type a higher package or a lower daily roaming rate and the verdict can flip. The sample is a worked sum, not a tariff.

If the gigabytes used had been past the gigabytes included, the difference would be multiplied by the extra price you typed and added to the package. A top-up changes the total. Another block of data is a new package price, a new included amount, or both, and the old comparison does not survive the change. Type the offer you are actually looking at, including the top-up if you expect to buy one. Leaving yesterday's price in the form produces yesterday's comparison.

The phone, the plan, and the country

The phone has to be unlocked, and it has to be eSIM-capable. A handset locked to one carrier can reject another plan. A handset without eSIM hardware cannot install the plan no matter what the arithmetic says. This page cannot see your phone. Coverage is per plan. A plan bought for one country is not automatically cover for the next border, and a regional plan is cover only for the places that plan names. Read the plan for the country you will actually be in. If the trip crosses a border, read the plan for the next country as well, or type a second offer and compare that bill on its own.

  • Roaming total equals days times the daily price. Ten days at 8 is 80 in the example above.
  • eSIM total equals the package plus extra gigabytes times the extra price. Extra gigabytes cannot go below zero.
  • Three gigabytes used against five included leaves the example package at 25.
  • The 55 gap belongs to those typed numbers. It is not a stored Airalo price.
  • A top-up means you type the new offer. The previous total is then out of date.

Questions people ask

How is the roaming total calculated?

Roaming is days multiplied by the daily price you type. Ten days at 8 per day is 80. The page does not add a tax or a fee you left out of that daily price. If the offer bundles some days and then charges another rate, type the figures that match it. The currency symbol is yours. Nothing on the page converts one currency into another.

Why is the example eSIM cheaper by 55?

Only because those numbers were typed. Ten days at 8 is a roaming total of 80. A package of 25 with 5 GB included and 3 GB used has no extra data, so the eSIM total stays 25. Eighty minus 25 is 55. The page does not know Airalo's current country price and does not claim that 25 or 8 is a shelf price. A different daily rate or package can shrink, grow, or flip the gap.

What if I use more data than the package includes?

The extra is gigabytes used minus gigabytes included, and it cannot fall below zero. That extra is multiplied by the extra price you typed and added to the package. Inside the included amount, the total is just the package. A top-up changes the total, so type the new package price and the new included amount. The page will not guess which top-up you meant.

Will any phone accept the eSIM?

No. The phone has to be unlocked and eSIM-capable. A carrier lock can refuse a second plan, and a handset without the hardware cannot install one. This page cannot see your device. Coverage is per plan. A plan for one country is not automatically cover for the next country on the route. Read the offer for the place you will actually be, then type its numbers.

Does this page know Airalo's price for my country?

No. No live Airalo tariff is stored here. The review describes what to check in an offer and does not freeze a country price, because those prices change. Open Airalo, find the plan for the country you will actually be in, and copy the package price, the included gigabytes, and the extra gigabyte price into the form. Until you do that, the result is only the arithmetic of whatever was already in the boxes, including the sample numbers.
