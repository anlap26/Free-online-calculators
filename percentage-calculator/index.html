Percentage calculator

Three percentage questions, each one division, with a zero denominator refused instead of printed as infinity.

Percent change divides by the starting number. A change in percentage points is a different question.

What is A percent of B is A divided by 100, times B. Twenty percent of 80 is 0.20 times 80, which is 16. The result is in the same units as B. The page does not add a currency symbol, because B might be people, grams, or a price. A is what percent of B is A divided by B. Twenty-five out of 200 is 0.125, which the page prints as 12.5 percent. Percent change from A to B is B minus A, divided by A. From 80 to 100 the change is 20 divided by 80, which is 0.25, or 25 percent. A fall from 100 to 80 is 20 divided by 100 the other way, which is minus 20 percent. The starting number is the denominator on purpose.

A zero denominator is refused. What percent of zero, and the percent change from zero, have no answer in ordinary arithmetic. The page says so instead of showing an infinity symbol. Negative inputs are allowed, because a loss and a signed measure are still numbers. The three questions are separate modes so a single pair of boxes is not silently reinterpreted. Switching the mode and pressing calculate uses the same A and B under the new question. Twenty and 80 as a percent-of question is 16. The same pair as a percent-change question is a change from 20 to 80, which is 300 percent, not 16. Read the mode before you read the result.

Percentage points are not this page

A move from 20 percent to 30 percent is a change of 10 percentage points and a percent change of 50 percent, because 10 divided by 20 is one half. If you type 20 and 30 into the percent-change mode, the page reports 50 percent. It does not report 10. If you wanted the percentage-point gap, subtract the two numbers yourself. Margin and markup are also different percentages of different bases. Margin divides the gap by the price. Markup divides the same gap by the cost. Those live on the margin page so this page can stay a plain percentage.

Fractions are exact integers on the fraction page. A percentage is a decimal. One half is 50 percent, and 1 divided by 2 is 0.5, but a repeating percentage such as one third is 33.333... percent and the page rounds the display. The fraction page keeps one third as 1/3. Standard deviation is about a list of numbers, not about a pair. Use this page when there are two numbers and a percent question, and the other page when there is a list.

What the division leaves out

  • No tax, tip, or discount is assumed. A discount is just "what is A percent of B" if you type it that way.
  • Compound growth is not a single percent change over many periods.
  • The display rounds. The operation itself is ordinary floating-point division.

Exact integer ratios are on the fraction calculator. A list of numbers is the standard deviation calculator. Cost and price percentages are the margin and markup calculator.

Questions people ask

What is 20 percent of 80?

16. The page divides 20 by 100 and multiplies by 80.

What percent is 25 of 200?

12.5 percent. The page divides 25 by 200.

What is the percent change from 80 to 100?

25 percent. The page divides 100 minus 80 by 80. From 100 to 80 the change is minus 20 percent.

Why is a move from 20 percent to 30 percent not 10?

Ten is the gap in percentage points. The percent change divides that gap by the starting number, and 10 / 20 is 50 percent.
