Triangle calculator

Area from two legs, from three sides that can form a triangle, or from a base and a height.

Each side of a three-sided triangle has to be shorter than the other two added together.

A right triangle is determined by its two legs. The hypotenuse is the square root of the sum of the squares of the legs. Legs of 3 and 4 give a hypotenuse of 5, because 9 + 16 is 25. The area is half the product of the legs, which is 6. The perimeter is 3 + 4 + 5, which is 12. The page does not ask which angle is the right angle. It treats the two numbers you type as the sides that meet at the right angle. If you have a hypotenuse and one leg, this mode is the wrong one. Solve the missing leg yourself with the same sum of squares, then come back if you want the area checked.

Three sides use Heron's formula. The semi-perimeter s is half of a + b + c. The area is the square root of s times (s − a) times (s − b) times (s − c). For 3, 4, and 5 the semi-perimeter is 6, and the product inside the root is 6 × 1 × 2 × 3, which is 36, and the root is 6. That matches the right-triangle area, which is the point of using a 3-4-5 set in both modes. Sides that fail the triangle inequality are rejected. One, two, and three add in pairs to a number that is not strictly greater than the third side: 1 + 2 equals 3. The area of that flat figure is not reported as zero. A zero or negative length is rejected in every mode.

Base and height

The third mode is base times height, divided by two. A base of 10 and a height of 4 give an area of 20. The height has to be the perpendicular height that belongs to that base, not the length of another side. The page cannot see your sketch. If you type a slanted side into the height box, the area will be wrong and the formula will not notice. This mode does not report a perimeter, because two numbers do not determine the other sides.

Units are whatever you typed. An area in centimetres comes from sides in centimetres. The page does not convert. The fraction page will reduce a ratio of two integers, which is a different job from an area. A list of side lengths is not a standard deviation problem unless that is actually what you wanted. The mean of 3, 4, and 5 is 4, and that number is not the area.

What the formulas assume

  • The right-triangle mode assumes a right angle between the two legs.
  • Heron's formula assumes a triangle that is not flat and not inside-out.
  • Base and height assume the height is perpendicular to the base.

Integer ratios are on the fraction calculator. Percent questions are on the percentage calculator. A list and its spread are on the standard deviation calculator.

Questions people ask

What is the hypotenuse of a 3-4-5 triangle?

5. The square root of 3 squared plus 4 squared is 5. The area is 6 and the perimeter is 12.

How does Heron's formula work here?

The semi-perimeter is half the sum of the sides. The area is the square root of s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c). For 3, 4, and 5 that root is 6.

Why is 1, 2, 3 rejected?

The sides are not strictly longer in pairs than the third side. 1 + 2 equals 3, so the figure is flat. The page does not report an area of zero for it.

Does base and height need a right angle?

The height has to be perpendicular to the base you typed. A slanted side in the height box is not the height.
