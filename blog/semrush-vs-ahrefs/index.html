Semrush vs Ahrefs vs free SEO tools

Semrush and Ahrefs answer market questions. Search Console answers what your site already received. A browser checker answers what is on the draft in front of you.

People ask which SEO suite to buy as if the two famous names were substitutes down to the button. They are not. Semrush and Ahrefs both keep large indexes of keywords, pages, and links, and both sell a subscription that a small site feels immediately. They do not share one database. A difficulty score in one product is not the difficulty score in the other. A backlink count in one is not a backlink count in the other. Treating the two dashboards as a single truth is how a spreadsheet argument starts.

This page will not crown a winner. It will not quote a price, a crawl size, or a user total. Those numbers change and they are marketing until you have read the current page. What it will do is separate three layers of work, so you stop paying a suite for a job a free tool already does, and you stop asking a free tool for a job that needs an index. The Semrush-specific method for a new site is in the keyword guide. How to spend a trial without wasting it is in the trial guide. The general tour is how to use Semrush.

The three layers

The first layer is your own site. Google Search Console shows queries and pages that already received impressions, if the property is verified. Bing has its own webmaster tools. Your host's logs, if you can read them, show requests. None of that is a picture of the market you have not entered. A brand new domain often has an empty Console. That emptiness is not a bug. It means you do not have a history yet.

The second layer is the draft. Word count, a keyword repeated too often, a title that will be cut, a contrast failure, a sitemap that lists the wrong URLs. Those checks do not need Semrush or Ahrefs. They need the page source. On this site they are the text analyzer, the meta tag generator, the robots and sitemap generator, and the contrast checker. They run locally. They do not see who else ranks.

The third layer is the market you are not in yet. Which phrases exist, which pages already occupy them, what the product thinks about difficulty, which domains link to the pages you are studying. That layer is why Semrush and Ahrefs exist. Free keyword planners exist too, and they are shaped by the ad auction. A planner that will not show a number until you spend, or that groups phrases into broad buckets, is a different instrument. Use it for ads. Do not pretend it is a content index.

What people actually compare

Keyword research is the overlap everyone feels. Both suites expand a seed, show a volume model, and attach a difficulty model. Use one product's numbers inside that product. Do not average a Semrush difficulty with an Ahrefs difficulty and call the mean a fact. If the two tools disagree, the useful next step is to open the search results yourself and read the pages, not to hunt a third score.

Backlinks are the other overlap. Ahrefs built its name on a link index. Semrush has a link index too. Neither shows you every link on the web, and neither can see a link that was never crawled. A lost link in a report is a prompt to look, not a proof that the link never existed. For a new site the link report matters less than the page you are about to publish, until you are studying why an older page is hard to move.

Site audit and position tracking are closer to operations. An audit crawls your URLs and lists issues. A tracker asks, on a schedule, where a phrase sits in a chosen country. Both need a project and a limit. A free browser extension that colours a result is not a tracker. It is a glance. If you will not look at the tracker next month, do not pay for one this month.

Content and advertising reports are where the products feel least alike, and where a trial is the only honest test. Read the module list on each pricing page. If a feature you need is absent from the plan you can afford, the brand name does not matter. The ROAS calculator on this site is the arithmetic after you know spend and revenue. It does not import either suite.

Free tools that are worth keeping even if you subscribe

Search Console stays. It is the record of your property, not a sample of the market. A suite can estimate a ranking. Console can show a query you already appeared for. When the two disagree, believe Console about your clicks and believe neither of them about a phrase you have never published.

The browser tools on this site stay too. Paying Semrush does not count the words in a paragraph. It does not escape a quote in a title tag. It does not build the UTM string you paste into a newsletter. Doing those jobs inside a paid suite, if it even offers them, is a slow way to edit a file. Draft locally. Research in the suite. Publish on the host.

A page builder's SEO panel is a form for a title and a description. It is not an index. Filling it without a phrase you chose on purpose is how sites grow a pile of near-duplicate titles. Generate the tags here, after the phrase is chosen, and paste them. The generator warns when the title is long. It does not know if the phrase is worth ranking for.

How to choose without a lab

If you have never used either suite, do not buy both. Run the trial you can actually finish. The method in the trial guide is written for Semrush because these guides are written around that suite. The same discipline applies if you trial Ahrefs instead: one country, one domain, an export before the window closes, and a written note of what was locked. A weekend of clicking is not a test.

Choose Semrush if the workflow you liked was the one that combined keyword lists, a crawl of your own site, and a place to keep the project together, and if the current plan includes those modules at a limit you will not hit in the first month. Choose Ahrefs if the work you actually did in a trial was link research and the Semrush link reports felt like the wrong shape for that job. Choose neither if your only need is a title, a word count, and Search Console. Paying to feel equipped is a real expense. The token bill for drafts is another real expense. Add them only after a page is scheduled.

Residency and tax on the invoice are your problem, not a feature comparison. A tool that bills in a currency your card punishes is more expensive than the headline. Read the checkout. This article will not invent the fee.

What neither suite will do

Neither will write a page that deserves to rank. Neither will make a thin page thick by revealing a magic score. Neither will tell you your server's response time. Speed, images, and contrast are on this site as separate tools, and hosting is a separate decision covered beside Cloudways and Kinsta. Neither suite is a substitute for reading the results page and asking whether you can honestly answer the query better than what is already there, or at least as clearly for a reader the current pages ignore.

If you want the suite these guides are written around, that name is Semrush. This page does not link to Semrush or to Ahrefs. Look each one up on its own domain when you compare checkouts. The calculators do not change either way.

Questions people ask

Is Semrush better than Ahrefs?

This page does not rank them. They keep different indexes. A difficulty score in one is not the score in the other. Trial the workflow you will actually repeat, and read the current plan before you pay.

Can free tools replace a suite?

They replace the draft layer: word counts, titles, robots files, contrast. They do not replace an index of phrases and links, and Search Console does not show a market you have not entered.

Should I trust keyword volume?

Treat it as the product's model, dated and tied to a country. Do not average Semrush and Ahrefs into one number. Open the results and read the pages.

Does this site earn from both?

No. This article does not link to Semrush or to Ahrefs. Ahrefs is named so you can check it on your own. There is no partner button here.

Where do I track my own clicks?

Google Search Console, on a verified property. A suite can estimate rankings. Console shows what your pages already received.
