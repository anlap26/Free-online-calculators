Affiliate disclosure

Some pages link to a company that may pay the publisher a commission if you open an account, buy a plan, or use a tracked link. You should not pay more because the link is an affiliate link. If a partner's checkout adds a fee, that fee is theirs, not a mark-up added by this site. The calculators do not change a result because a link is present.

Where the links are

Every partner button reads assets/js/affiliates.js. The keys are nordvpn, surfshark, proton, onepassword, bitwarden, genki, interactivebrokers, binance, bybit, ledger, trezor, semrush, airalo, wise, cloudways, kinsta, and amazon. A value that still begins with #AFFILIATE_ is a placeholder. Replace it with the full URL from the affiliate network, save the file, and publish. You do not need to edit each page. Links open in a new tab with rel="sponsored noopener". Amazon links use rel="sponsored nofollow noopener".

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Those links are searches on Amazon.de with the tag in assets/js/affiliates.js. They are not links to a made-up product code.

What we will and will not claim

Guides describe what a product is for and what a buyer should verify. They do not invent a price, a speed ranking, a customer count, or a medical outcome. Plans change. A product may be unavailable where you live. A VPN does not make a person anonymous in every sense. A hardware wallet does not help if the recovery phrase is stored in a screenshot. Insurance pages are not a policy and not a promise that a claim will be paid.

If you are a partner or a reader

A partner relationship does not buy a changed formula. If a sentence on a guide is wrong, the correction path is a GitHub issue on anlap26/free-online-calculators. This note was written on 19 November 2026.
