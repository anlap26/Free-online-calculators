About

Free Online Calculators is a static site of tools for money, privacy, relocation, and web work. It is published by Anna, who also publishes MyCyTax, a separate site about Cyprus property and tax. The two sites do not share a database. This one has no database.

Each calculator is a page with one job. The formula is on the page. The numbers are checked against known cases in the repository tests: compound growth, a 90-day Schengen window, margin versus markup, contrast of black on white, and an AES round trip. If a result looks wrong, the useful next step is the formula on the page, not a second opinion from the same script.

Affiliate links pay for the time spent on the site if a partner pays a commission. They are labelled on the page and collected in the disclosure. The partner address itself lives only in assets/js/affiliates.js. The pages are rebuilt from that file. A name without a live URL is not a button.

Corrections to a formula or a translation can be sent as an issue on the GitHub repository anlap26/free-online-calculators. The site does not offer personal financial, tax, legal, or immigration advice.
