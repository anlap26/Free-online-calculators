Open Semrush

Paste the draft into the box. The count runs in the browser, and the text is not uploaded. Words are the pieces left after the text is trimmed and split on whitespace. Two spaces do not make an extra word. A hyphenated form stays one word if you did not put a space in it. Characters are Unicode code points, which is what you get from walking the string one code point at a time. A character count with spaces and a count without spaces are both shown. An emoji that uses two code points counts as two, because that is the length the script measures, not a guess about what a person calls one character.

Sentences are split on a full stop, an exclamation mark, or a question mark. A piece counts as a sentence only when it still contains a letter or a number after the split. A line of dots does not become several sentences. Reading time is the word count divided by 200. Speaking time is the word count divided by 130. Both answers are in minutes. They describe a steady pace, not your reader and not a stage. A technical paragraph can take longer to read than the 200-word pace, and the page will not adjust for that.

How density is counted

Keyword density is the number of non-overlapping hits, times the number of words in the phrase, divided by the word count of the whole text. The search walks the lowercased text and, after each hit, continues after the phrase, so the same characters are not counted twice. Take the phrase new york inside the sentence new york and new york. That is two hits. The sentence has five words. The phrase is two words, so the numerator is 2 times 2, which is 4, and the density is 4 divided by 5. A one-word keyword in the same sentence would use a numerator of hits times 1.

Density is a diagnostic. It tells you how large a share of the words the phrase occupies. It is not a ranking factor you can stuff. Repeating a phrase until the percentage looks familiar does not make a page deserve a query, and this tool does not pretend that it does. If the phrase is absent, the count is zero. If the box is empty of words, density stays at zero rather than dividing by zero.

Top words skip a small stopword list

Beside the counts, the page lists frequent words. A short stopword list is skipped, so words such as the, and, and for do not fill the list in English, and a matching list of function words is skipped in Russian. The list is small and fixed in the script. It is not a judgment about which words matter to a reader. A word shorter than two characters is skipped as well. The result is a hint about repetition in this draft, nothing more.

Questions people ask

How is a word counted?

The text is trimmed and split on whitespace. Repeated spaces do not add words. The script does not look words up in a dictionary.

Why is density 4/5 for new york and new york?

There are two non-overlapping hits, the phrase is two words, and the sentence is five words. Two times two, divided by five, is 4/5.

Is a higher density better for ranking?

No. Density is a diagnostic of how much of the draft the phrase occupies. Stuffing the phrase is not a ranking method this tool supports.

Is my text uploaded?

No. The counts run in your browser. The page does not send the draft to a server.

What do the reading and speaking times assume?

Reading time is words divided by 200. Speaking time is words divided by 130. Both are steady paces, not a measurement of your reader.
