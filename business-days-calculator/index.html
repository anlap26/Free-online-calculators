Business days calculator

Calendar days between two dates exclude the start and include the end. Business days are the Mondays to Fridays in that span, minus the weekday holidays you list.

A due date is not a transfer speed

People count business days because an invoice has a due date and a payout has a timing. Wise moves money across currencies. This page does not know how long a given transfer takes and will not invent a speed.

The span between two dates is counted in UTC calendar dates. The start day is excluded and the end day is included, which is the ordinary way to say how many days you step forward. Friday to the following Monday is three calendar days: Saturday, Sunday, and Monday. The Friday you started on is not one of them. If you put the later date first, the range is rejected rather than silently swapped, so a reversed pair does not look like a short trip.

Business days are the Monday-to-Friday dates inside that same span. Saturday and Sunday are weekends. A holiday counts only when it falls on a weekday you listed. Weekends are not also counted as holidays. If you type a Saturday into the holiday list, it stays a weekend in the weekend tally and does not appear again as a holiday, and it is not subtracted a second time. A weekday you list is removed from the business-day count and shown in the holiday count. The list is yours. The page does not know public holidays for a country, and it will not invent a national calendar.

A Friday, a Monday, and a holiday

Friday 20 November 2026 to Monday 23 November 2026 is 3 calendar days and 1 business day. The business day is the Monday. Add 1 business day to Friday 20 November 2026 and you land on Monday 23 November 2026. The counter walks forward one UTC date at a time, skips Saturday and Sunday, and stops when it has collected the number of business days you asked for. A holiday on that Monday pushes the landing day to Tuesday 24 November. The Monday is a real calendar day. It is simply not a business day once you listed it. Adding zero business days stays on the start date. The walk does not treat the start date itself as a day you have added.

Dates are parsed as UTC dates from the year, month, and day you pick. A time zone cannot shift the calendar day, because the page never converts a local midnight into UTC and back. 20 November is 20 November whether the browser is in a zone that is hours ahead or hours behind. An impossible day, such as 31 November, is rejected. The date control on the form already speaks in calendar dates. The arithmetic keeps that promise.

This is not the Schengen count

A stay under the Schengen 90-in-180 rule counts each calendar day you are present, and both the entry day and the exit day count. That is a different question from the number of business days between two dates, and this page does not answer it. Use the Schengen stay calculator for days of presence in a window. Do not paste a business-day total into a visa question. A meeting across zones, if the issue is the clock rather than the count of days, belongs on the meeting planner, which still will not turn a holiday into a working hour.

Invoice due dates and payout timing are why people count business days. A contract that says ten business days means this kind of walk, once you have listed the holidays the contract actually names. Wise is one way to collect or send the money the invoice describes. How long a particular transfer takes is on Wise's own page for that corridor and that moment. This calculator does not know it and will not print a speed. The margin on the invoice, if you still need it, is the margin calculator, which is also silent on transfer time.

Questions people ask

How many days from Friday 20 November 2026 to Monday 23 November 2026?

3 calendar days and 1 business day. The start is excluded, the end is included, and the weekend is not a business day.

What if I add one business day to that Friday and Monday is a holiday?

Without the holiday you land on Monday 23 November 2026. A holiday on that Monday pushes the result to Tuesday 24 November.

Does listing Saturday subtract it twice?

No. Weekends are counted as weekends. A holiday removes a weekday. A Saturday in the list is not also a holiday in the holiday tally.

Can my time zone change the date?

No. The page reads year, month, and day as a UTC date. Local midnight is not converted, so the calendar day does not shift.

Is this the Schengen day count?

No. Schengen counts days of presence, including entry and exit. This tool counts a span of calendar days and the business days inside it.
