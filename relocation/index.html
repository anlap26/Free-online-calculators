Relocation and remote-work calculators

Day counts, a clearly labelled cost index, a time-zone planner, roaming versus eSIM, and a freelance rate that includes tax and days you cannot bill.

The stay calculator has two counts. Schengen mode marks each calendar day in your trips once, then asks how many of those days fall in the 180-day window ending on the date you pick. Entry day and exit day both count. A day over 90 is flagged. The year mode counts distinct days inside a calendar year, which is the shape of the 183-day test many countries publish. It is not the US substantial-presence formula, and it is not a reading of a tax treaty. Overlapping trips are not double counted.

The cost page uses a rounded index with New York at 100. The figures are editorial, last reviewed on 19 November 2026, and stored in one file you can edit. They are not rents from a statistical office. A salary in city A is scaled by the ratio of the indexes. The meeting planner converts a clock time in one IANA zone into the others and marks who is inside 09:00 to 17:00. The eSIM page subtracts a package you type from roaming you type. It does not know this week's Airalo price. The freelance page grosses up a net income for tax and business costs, then divides by hours left after vacation, sick days, holidays, and non-billable time.

Insurance and eSIM guides sit next to these tools because a day count does not tell you which policy covers a routine doctor, and a roaming total does not tell you whether your phone accepts an eSIM.

Travel partners

The day count, the cost index, and the eSIM sum do not book a trip. Airalo sells travel eSIMs. Aviasales searches flights. Klook lists tours and activities. Tiqets sells attraction tickets. Prices change. The calculators do not read those prices.

Questions people ask

Does 90 days in this tool mean I can enter?

It means the days you listed are under or over 90 in that window. Border rules, visas, and previous stays you forgot to list are outside the form.

Where do the cost indexes come from?

They are rounded editorial comparisons, New York = 100, reviewed 19 November 2026. The file assets/js/cost-of-living.js is the source. They are not an official price survey.

Does the freelance rate include income tax?

It grosses up by the tax percent you type. It does not know your brackets, your country's social contributions, or what you can deduct.
