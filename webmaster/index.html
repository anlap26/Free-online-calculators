Webmaster and content tools

Checks and generators for a page you are about to publish: text, tags, files, colour, ad maths, and a policy draft you still have to read.

The token calculator multiplies tokens by prices you can edit. The starter table is a placeholder dated in the price file, not a quote from a lab. The text tool counts words, characters, sentences, reading time at 200 words a minute, and keyword density as hits times words in the phrase, divided by all words. The meta tool writes title, description, Open Graph, Twitter, and a small JSON-LD block, and it warns when the title or description is long enough to be cut.

Page-speed revenue is a model, not a measurement. You type visits, the conversion you have now, revenue per conversion, and how much of that conversion you assume each extra second removes. Robots and sitemap text is generated from paths you list. Image compression redraws the bitmap on a canvas and never uploads it. UTM builds a query string and a QR code, with optional logo space covered by high error correction. JSON, CSV, and SQL are formatted locally. ROAS, margin, contrast, and the pixel converter are pure arithmetic. The policy generator writes a draft from checkboxes. It tells you to delete any sentence that is not true.

Hosting and SEO guides sit beside the speed and text tools. They describe how the products differ. They do not publish a benchmark from a test we did not run.

WCAG contrast checker

Contrast is the lighter relative luminance plus 0.05, divided by the darker one plus 0.05. You type both colours. The page does not know your font size.

PX to REM converter

rem is the pixels divided by the root font size. em uses the parent. vw is the pixels divided by the viewport width, times 100.

Margin and markup calculator

Margin divides the gap by the price. Markup divides the same gap by the cost. The two percentages are not interchangeable, and mixing them underprices the work.

Business days calculator

Questions people ask

Are the model prices current?

No. They are editable placeholders. The file assets/js/llm-prices.js says when they were filled in. Replace them from the vendor's page.

Does image compression upload my file?

No. The browser resizes and encodes it. The download is produced locally.

Is the privacy policy legally sufficient?

It is a draft that matches the boxes you tick. It is not a legal opinion. Read it and remove anything your site does not do.
