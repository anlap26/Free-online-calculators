GPA calculator

A credit-weighted average on one common 4.0 map. A+ does not rise above 4.0.

Schools that award 4.3 for A+, or that use a different step, will not match this map.

Each row is a letter and a number of credits. The grade points for the row are the letter's value times the credits. The GPA is the sum of grade points divided by the sum of credits. The map is A+ = 4.0, A = 4.0, A− = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B− = 2.7, C+ = 2.3, C = 2.0, C− = 1.7, D+ = 1.3, D = 1.0, D− = 0.7, and F = 0. An A and a B, three credits each, are (4×3 + 3×3) / 6, which is 3.5. An A− worth four credits and an F worth two credits are (3.7×4 + 0×2) / 6, which is 3.7×4/6, about 2.467. Empty credit rows are skipped. A credit count of zero with no letter is skipped. Negative credits are rejected. A letter that is not on the map is rejected.

A+ and A share 4.0 because that is the cap on this particular scale. A school that prints 4.3 or 4.33 for A+ is using a different scale, and typing A+ here will not reproduce that transcript. Weighted high-school GPA, where an honours course adds a point, is also a different scale. This page has no honours switch. If a course is worth more, give it more credits only when that is actually how the school weights it. Do not invent a credit to smuggle in a bonus the map does not have.

How the rows add

You can add up to twenty courses. Removing a row is blocked when it is the last one, so the form always has a place to type. The credits line in the result is the sum of the credits that were included. The grade-points line is the numerator, so you can divide it by the credits and recover the GPA. A course with zero credits adds nothing to either total. That is useful for a placeholder row and useless as a way to raise the average. The percentage page will not weight letters. The grade page on this site weights raw points and then maps 90, 80, 70, and 60 onto A, B, C, and D. It is not this GPA. A 3.5 GPA is not "87.5 percent" unless a school published that conversion, and this page does not invent one.

Pass/fail courses are not on the map. Leaving them off the form is the honest move when they do not enter the GPA. Putting them in as an A or an F would change the average by a rule the school did not use. Transfer credits that arrive already averaged are a single row only if you know the letter and the credits. The page will not unpack a previous GPA into courses it cannot see.

What the map is not

  • It is not a 4.3 scale and not a 5.0 honours scale.
  • It is not a percent, and it does not convert to one.
  • It does not know which courses your school excludes.

Points earned out of points possible are on the grade calculator. A plain percentage is the percentage calculator. An exact ratio of two integers is the fraction calculator.

Questions people ask

What is the GPA of an A and a B, three credits each?

3.5. Grade points are 4×3 plus 3×3, which is 21, divided by 6 credits.

What is A+ worth?

4.0, the same as A. This scale does not go to 4.3.

What is A− worth?

3.7. Then B+ is 3.3, B is 3.0, B− is 2.7, and the steps continue down to F at 0.

Can I include a pass/fail course?

Only if your school actually folds it into the GPA as one of these letters. Otherwise leave it off. The page has no separate pass value.
