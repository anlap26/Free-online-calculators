Date calculator

The calendar gap between two dates, or a date shifted by years, then months, then days, with the end of the month clamped.

Years are applied first, then months, then days. 31 January plus one month is the last day of February.

The between mode reports four readings of the same pair of dates. Days from the first date to the second is the UTC midnight count, so 1 January to 31 January is 30. The inclusive count adds one, because both dates are being counted, and that pair is 31. Weeks is the day count divided by 7, so 30 days is 4 and 2/7 weeks, not a rounded 4. The year-month-day line is the same calendar subtraction the age page uses. If the second date is earlier, the numbers are shown with a minus and the span is still the positive gap. An impossible date is rejected.

The add mode starts from the date you type. Years are added first, and if that lands on a day the destination month does not have, the day clamps to the last real day. Months are added next, with the same clamp. Days are added last, and days can roll into the next month because a day is a real calendar step. 31 January 2026 plus one month is 28 February 2026. 31 January 2024 plus one month is 29 February 2024, because 2024 is a leap year. 1 March 2026 plus minus one day is 28 February 2026. The order matters. Adding the days before the months would not be the same operation, and the page does years, then months, then days.

How this differs from age and from business days

The age page is this calendar subtraction aimed at a birthday, including the 29 February rule for the next anniversary. This page will also clamp 29 February when you add years into a non-leap February. Adding one year to 29 February 2024 lands on 28 February 2025, because the year step clamps. That is a different sentence from "the birthday anniversary is 1 March," which is what the age page says about completing a whole year. Use the page whose sentence matches the question. Business days skip Saturdays, Sundays, and the holidays you list. This page does not. A weekend in the middle of the span still counts.

Negative years, months, and days are allowed and mean subtract. They have to be whole numbers. A half month is not a calendar operation this page will invent. Time of day is the time calculator. Two clock times on the same date are zero days here and a duration there.

What the clamp is for

  • It keeps 31 January plus one month inside February.
  • It does not mean February has a 31st that was hidden.
  • Inclusive days and the gap in days answer different questions. Both are labelled.

Birthdays are on the age calculator. Hours are on the time calculator. Weekdays are on the business days calculator.

Questions people ask

How many days are there from 1 January to 31 January?

30 days from the first date to the second. The count that includes both dates is 31.

What is 31 January plus one month?

The last day of February: the 28th, or the 29th in a leap year. The day is clamped to the month instead of spilling into March.

In what order are years, months, and days added?

Years first, then months, then days. Days can roll into the next month. Months clamp.

Does this skip weekends?

No. Every calendar day counts. Weekdays with holidays removed are on the business-days page.
