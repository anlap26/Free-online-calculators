Math calculators

Percentages, fractions, a standard deviation you can recompute from the squared deviations, and the area of a triangle.

The percentage page answers three questions. What is A percent of B is A divided by 100, times B. A is what percent of B is A divided by B. Percent change from A to B is B minus A, divided by A. A zero starting value has no percent change, and the page says so instead of printing infinity.

Fractions stay in integers. One half plus one third is five sixths, because the common denominator is six and the numerators become three and two. The result is reduced by the greatest common divisor and also shown as a mixed number. A zero denominator, or dividing by a zero fraction, is rejected. Inputs have to be integers inside the safe integer range so the cross-multiplication does not silently round.

Standard deviation uses the set you type. The mean is the sum divided by the count. Population variance divides the sum of squared deviations by the count. Sample variance divides by the count minus one, which needs at least two numbers. The famous set 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9 has mean 5, population variance 4, and population standard deviation 2. Triangle area is half the product of the legs for a right triangle, Heron's formula for three sides that satisfy the triangle inequality, or base times height divided by two.

Questions people ask

Is percent change the same as a percentage point?

No. Percent change divides the difference by the starting number. A move from 20 percent to 30 percent is a 10 percentage-point change and a 50 percent change. This page does the second one when you type 20 and 30.

Why are there two standard deviations?

Population variance divides by the count. Sample variance divides by the count minus one. Use the sample line when the numbers are a sample from a larger set. Use the population line when the numbers are the whole set.

What if three lengths cannot form a triangle?

The page rejects them. Each side has to be shorter than the other two added together. A 1, 2, 3 set is flat, so the area is not reported as zero by accident.
