Time calculator

Durations you can add and subtract, and the gap between two clock times, including one that crosses midnight.

Clock times use a 24-hour clock. An end that is earlier than the start is the next day, and the result says so.

First duration

Second duration

A duration is hours, minutes, and seconds added into a single count of seconds. Adding two durations adds those counts. Subtracting takes the second away from the first. The result is carried back into days, hours, minutes, and seconds, with a minus when the result is negative. One hour and 30 minutes, plus 2 hours, 45 minutes, and 30 seconds, is 4 hours, 15 minutes, and 30 seconds. One hour minus 2 hours and 30 minutes is minus 1 hour and 30 minutes. The page does not hide the minus inside a "borrow" story that pretends the result is still positive. Minutes and seconds at or above 60 are normal inputs. They are carried: 90 minutes is 1 hour and 30 minutes inside the total, not an error.

The clock mode takes a start hour and minute and an end hour and minute, on a 24-hour clock. Hours have to be from 0 to 23 and minutes from 0 to 59. The duration is the end minus the start in minutes. If the end is earlier than the start, 24 hours are added and the result says the count crossed midnight. From 22:00 to 06:00 is 8 hours. From 09:15 to 17:45 is 8 hours and 30 minutes, and that one does not cross midnight. The mode does not know a time zone. Two civil times on a day when the clocks change can be 23 or 25 hours apart in elapsed time. This page treats every hour as 60 minutes, which is the clock-face question, not the elapsed-UTC question.

What belongs on the date page instead

A gap of several days is the date calculator. This page will add a duration longer than a day and show the day count in the result, so 30 hours is 1 day and 6 hours. It will not tell you which calendar date that lands on. Age is dates without a time of day. A meeting across time zones is the world-time planner, which uses IANA zones and the offset of a particular date, including daylight saving. This page does not. If you need 15:00 in London as a New York clock time, the planner is the page that knows the offset. If you need how long a shift is, this page is enough.

Negative clock inputs outside 0 to 23 are rejected. A duration field can be negative if you type a minus, and subtraction already covers that case more clearly. Zero is a valid duration. The result of adding zero is the first duration, carried into hours, minutes, and seconds.

What the clock does not know

  • No time zone and no daylight-saving transition.
  • No date. A 30-hour result is a duration, not a calendar landing.
  • Seconds are not part of the clock-to-clock mode. They are part of the duration modes.

Calendar gaps are on the date calculator. Birthdays are on the age calculator. A time in one zone shown in another is the world time meeting planner.

Questions people ask

What is 1:30:00 plus 2:45:30?

4:15:30. The page adds the seconds and carries 60 seconds into a minute and 60 minutes into an hour.

What is 1 hour minus 2 hours 30 minutes?

Minus 1 hour and 30 minutes. The sign stays visible.

How long is it from 22:00 to 06:00?

8 hours. The end is earlier than the start, so the page adds 24 hours and says the count crosses midnight.

Does this handle daylight saving?

No. Every hour is 60 minutes. A civil day that is 23 or 25 hours long is a time-zone question, and the meeting planner is the page for zones.
