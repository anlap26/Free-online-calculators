Age calculator

Whole years, then whole months, then the remaining days, from a date of birth to the date you pick.

The day count is in UTC, so a daylight-saving change does not add or remove a day.

Age is the calendar gap from the date of birth to the as-of date. Whole years come first. If the as-of month and day are before the birthday in that year, the year count is one smaller and the months borrow. If the day of the month is before the birth day, the month count borrows, and the days are filled from the length of the previous month. Someone born on 15 January 2000 is, on 25 November 2026, 26 years, 10 months, and 10 days. The total-days line is the number of midnights between the two UTC dates. It does not depend on the time zone of the browser and it does not grow or shrink when the clocks change.

A birthday of 29 February is the awkward case. In a leap year the anniversary is 29 February. In a non-leap year February has no 29th, so the next date on which the year, month, and day counts land on a whole number of years is 1 March. Born on 29 February 2000, the age on 29 February 2024 is 24 years, 0 months, and 0 days. On 28 February 2025 the year count is still 24. On 1 March 2025 it is 25 years, 0 months, and 0 days, and the next-birthday line is that same 1 March with zero days to wait. The page states that rule because other calculators pick 28 February instead. Both are conventions. This one follows the calendar subtraction.

The date the form starts with

The birth field starts at 15 June 1990 so the form is not empty. The as-of field is filled with today's date in your browser when the page loads, and you can replace it. An as-of date before the birth date is rejected. An impossible date, such as 31 April, is rejected because it is not a calendar day. The business-days page is a different count: it skips weekends and the holidays you list. This page counts every calendar day. The time page counts hours. Age does not have a time of day. A birth at 23:00 and a birthday at 01:00 are the same date here.

The date calculator adds months and clamps the end of the month. This page does not add a duration to a birthday except to find the next anniversary. If you want "what date is 40 years after this birth," use the date calculator, and remember that 29 February plus a non-leap year of months is handled there by clamping, which is a related but separate rule.

What the count is not

  • It is not a legal age for a contract, a licence, or a benefit.
  • It does not know the time of birth.
  • It does not skip weekends.

Adding and subtracting dates is the date calculator. Hours and clock times are the time calculator. Weekdays with holidays removed are the business days calculator.

Questions people ask

How is age in years, months, and days calculated?

Whole years first, then whole months, then the remaining days, borrowing from the previous month when the day of the month has not been reached. 15 January 2000 to 25 November 2026 is 26 years, 10 months, and 10 days.

What happens to a 29 February birthday in a non-leap year?

The anniversary that completes a whole number of years is 1 March. On 28 February the year count has not stepped yet. On 1 March the months and days are zero.

Does daylight saving change the day count?

No. Total days are UTC midnights between the two dates. A 23-hour or 25-hour civil day does not change that count.

Is this a legal age?

No. It is the calendar gap between two dates you typed. A statute can define age differently.
