Relative luminance follows the sRGB formula. Each channel is turned into a linear light value: a channel at or below the sRGB breakpoint is divided by 12.92, and a channel above it is shifted and raised to 2.4. Luminance is then 0.2126 times red, plus 0.7152 times green, plus 0.0722 times blue. Contrast is the lighter of the two luminances plus 0.05, divided by the darker plus 0.05. The same pair in either order gives the same ratio, because the formula sorts lighter and darker itself. Black on white is 21 to 1. White on white is 1 to 1.

You type two colours as hex. The checker does not sample your screen, does not read a stylesheet, and does not know which element sits on which. If the text colour and the background colour are swapped in the fields, the ratio does not change. A colour written with a short hex or with a word such as black has to be a six-digit hex here. The page will say the value is not a colour it can read rather than guessing.

The thresholds, and what large text means

The usual WCAG cutoffs are applied to that ratio. AA for normal text is 4.5. AA for large text, and the cutoff used for UI components and graphical objects, is 3. AAA for normal text is 7. AAA for large text is 4.5. Large text in WCAG is 18 point, or 14 point bold. This tool does not know your font size, your weight, or your line height. It checks the two colours. You decide whether the text you are about to ship is large. A pass at 3 is not a pass for body copy.

Two greys are worth remembering because they sit on either side of the normal-text line. #767676 on white is just over 4.5, so it meets AA for normal text and misses AAA. #777777 on white is under 4.5, so it fails AA for normal text and still clears the large-text and UI line of 3. One step in the hex can cross the line. Do not round a grey by eye and assume the neighbour still passes.

The palette is a set of stops, not a finished theme

From the background colour the page builds lighter and darker stops. They are a starting set of related colours, not a palette that has been tested. You still have to run each pair you mean to use, text on background and background on text if both occur. A stop that looks calm next to the background can fail the 4.5 line. A stop that passes on white can fail on a tinted page. The list does not replace the check.

Contrast is also not a hosting question. Kinsta can serve the page. It cannot see the two hex values, and moving host will not lift a grey over 4.5. If the type size is what you are changing, the pixel converter turns a px length into rem from the root size you type. It still does not know whether that length counts as large text in WCAG. Pictures that sit behind type are a different problem. The compressor changes bytes. It does not score the contrast of text drawn on top of a photograph.

Questions people ask

How is contrast calculated?

Each colour becomes a relative luminance with the sRGB formula. Contrast is the lighter luminance plus 0.05, divided by the darker plus 0.05.

What is black on white?

21 to 1. White on white is 1 to 1. The order of the two fields does not change the ratio.

Does #777777 on white pass AA for body text?

No. It is under 4.5. #767676 on white is just over 4.5. Large text and UI components use 3, which both greys clear.

Does the checker know if my text is large?

No. WCAG large text is 18 point or 14 point bold. The tool only compares the two colours you type.

Are the palette stops already approved?

No. They are lighter and darker stops from the background. Test each pair you intend to ship.
